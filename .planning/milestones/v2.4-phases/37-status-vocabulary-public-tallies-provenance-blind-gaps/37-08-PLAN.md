<objective>
Close G-37-4, the one issue UAT raised for Phase 37, taking the developer's chosen option (b):
give `campaign_rollup()`/`get_or_compute_rollup()` the same live-unused split the per-run tally
already has.

The defect, as diagnosed (37-UAT.md `## Gaps`, authoritative): `get_or_compute_rollup()`
(`solsys_code/campaign_tally.py:613`) caches the WHOLE `campaign_rollup()` dict, the three
`unused_*` keys included, and returns the cached dict unchanged on a hit. The per-run path
deliberately does not -- `get_or_compute_tally()` and `tallies_for_runs()` cache WITHOUT a
computed unused figure and re-apply `_apply_unused_fields()` live on every call, cache hits
included (`campaign_tally.py:269, 278, 303, 308, 343, 349, 406`), precisely so the table's
Progress column and the calendar's `[U]` marker can never visibly disagree (CR-02, 37-REVIEW.md).
The roll-up strip sits directly above those Progress cells on the same page and does not follow
that rule, so one page response can contradict itself.

The scope is narrower than the first report, and the plan must not widen it:
`build_rollup_cache_key()` already folds in `campaign_records_version()`, so the strip is fully
live to a record-driven narrowing -- the roadmap's own trigger for "updating as the projector
narrows". The residual is confined to the two drivers of the unused figure that do not move
`records_version`: an awarded night elapsing past its projected sunrise (`is_unused_allocation_night()`
comparing `end_time` to `timezone.now()`, D-15) and a staff `run_status` edit into a
`RUN_STATUS_MARKER` status (D-14). Those two, and only those two, are what this plan makes live.

Purpose: Success Criterion 2 of Phase 37 -- any visitor sees a live tally of nights observed /
scheduled / expired-or-failed / unused, "and the campaign page rolls the same tally up across its
runs". A roll-up that disagrees with the rows it is rolling up is not a roll-up of them. D-15 asks
for surfaces that agree BY CONSTRUCTION, through one shared classifier, not surfaces that agree
eventually.

Output: one campaign-level unused applier holding D-10's counting rule, a cache that never carries
a computed unused figure, an end-to-end page test proving strip and rows move together, unit
coverage for both drivers on the cache-hit path, a relaxed-but-real query bound on the anonymous
campaign list, and two corrected docstrings plus the paired runbook paragraph.
