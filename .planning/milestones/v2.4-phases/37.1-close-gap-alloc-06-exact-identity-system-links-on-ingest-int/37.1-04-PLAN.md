<objective>
Close UAT gaps G-37.1-1 and G-37.1-1-nav: make the attribution page's Confirmed and Dismissed
sections actually open under Bootstrap 5, show the Confirmed section by default, and give staff a
route to the page that does not depend on anything waiting in the queue.

Purpose: phase 37.1 made an empty attribution queue the normal state and made system links (D-05,
"Confirmed by" = "System (exact match)") visible only in the Confirmed table, with their Undo
(D-06). In UAT the Confirmed and Dismissed sections never opened -- `attribution_queue.html` still
uses Bootstrap 4 collapse attributes while the TOM 3.0.1 base page loads Bootstrap 5 -- and the
only links to `/campaigns/attribution/` sit inside the orphan-count banner and the calendar event
form, so once the queue empties the page cannot be reached by navigation. Both together hid
exactly the rows 37.1 created.

Output: a fixed template with Confirmed open by default, a persistent staff-only Attribution
button on the campaign list, render-level and browser tests that would have caught the defect, a
template-wide guard against Bootstrap 4 data attributes, and the runbook updated.
</objective>
