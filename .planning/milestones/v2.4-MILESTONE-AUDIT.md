### Findings from the integration check

1. **System-link outcomes are not in the tick summary.** † `step_discovery` builds its
   `StepResult` from `(rows_swept, failed_count, failed_codes)` only. A system link that was
   skipped or failed shows as a captured INFO line, not in the summary an operator reads and
   not in the `failed` flag. SCHED-09's "visible to an operator" is thinner for the new step
   than for the others.
2. **A classical run with no target and no campaign** cannot be system-linked and is not
   offered in the attribution queue, which is campaign-gated; its night retires only through
   an admin edit. Consistent with ALLOC-06's "target or campaign membership". Recorded as a
   scope fact, by the checker's code read.
3. **`observation_projector.py:406`** logs a caught exception's message at WARNING. It dates
   from Phase 34 and the exceptions are local rather than portal calls, so a credential is
   unlikely, but the earlier audit did not list it.
4. **A second notion of "terminal"** at `backfill_lco_observations.py:471-476`, taken from the
   facility's own list rather than `classify_record`.
