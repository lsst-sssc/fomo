<objective>
Close the second half of UAT gap G-37.1-1-alloc: move the discovery sweep onto FOMO's block rule
from plan 37.1-07, give it the same one-time `--recheck-unscheduled` catch-up for records stored
under TOM's old rule, and deliver the paired notebook and runbook.

Purpose: `backfill_lco_observations` is the ingest path the unattended discovery step runs every
15 minutes. Today it copies TOM's COMPLETED-else-PENDING rule for embedded blocks
(`_select_block()`) and calls TOM's own `get_observation_status()` for live lookups, so a request
whose only block aborted after taking data is stored with no times and its allocation night never
retires. Its finished-record skip (F2) also rests on the premise that failed states never carry a
block -- false for an aborted block -- so records already stored that way are never looked at
again. The user decided on 2026-10-04 that aborted (and in-progress) blocks retire their night and
that existing records are re-resolved by re-running the existing backfill commands; the watched
proposals (KEY2026B-004 today) need the discovery command's form of that re-run.

Output: the sweep on `select_schedule_block()` and `FomoLCOFacility`; `--recheck-unscheduled`
threaded through the gate, `sweep_proposal()`, `sweep_watched_rows()` and the CLI (never through
the unattended runner); tests; the backfill demo notebook re-targeted, extended with an
aborted-block section and regenerated; the runbook's discovery section corrected.
</objective>
