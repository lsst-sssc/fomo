<objective>
Give every allocation — a `CampaignRun` with a resolved site and a resolved window, campaign or
no campaign — its own per-night sunset-to-sunrise calendar events under a new
`ALLOC:{run_pk}:{night}` key namespace owned by a new peer module
`solsys_code/allocation_projector.py`, dispatched to from `reconcile_run()` in place of the
retired `RUN:{pk}:{date}` per-night family, with the observation handoff (a linked, placed or
observed record retires its night; unlinking restores it) working end to end.

This is the phase's tracer plan: Task 1 is a `type="tracer"` slice that wires one path through
every layer this phase touches (shared night helper -> new projector module -> dispatch seam ->
calendar rows -> sweep summary) and proves it with a real end-to-end test; Tasks 2 and 3 expand
out from that proven slice.

Purpose: ALLOC-01/02/03. Everything later in the phase (sub-night windows, triggers, the
classical ingest rewrite, the cutover command) hangs off the module and the dispatch seam this
plan creates.

Output: `solsys_code/allocation_projector.py`, `solsys_code/tests/test_allocation_projector.py`,
a public `observing_night()` and `split_telescope_instrument()`, a rewritten `reconcile_run()`
dispatch, and two new `ReconcileResult` counters surfaced by the sweep.

Paired docs for this phase are owned by plan 35-07 (wave 5) — `load_telescope_runs_demo.ipynb`,
`reconcile_campaign_runs_demo.ipynb` and `docs/runbooks/telescope_runs_calendar.rst`. Nothing in
this plan changes documented operator behaviour on its own.
</objective>
