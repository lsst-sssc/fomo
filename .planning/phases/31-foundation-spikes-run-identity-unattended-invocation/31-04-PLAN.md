<objective>
Settle the unattended-invocation mechanism against verified facts about the real target host -
overlap prevention, credential handling, and how a missed invocation becomes visible - so Phase 34
implements something checked rather than something recommended.

Purpose: STATE.md flagged the absence of real host facts as the one gap only the operator could
close, and D-01 through D-04 closed the policy half of it: FOMO runs today on a local Rocky 9 or
WSL2 host, will eventually run in LCO's AWS Kubernetes cluster, and the mechanism is cron plus a
file lock inside the FOMO container rather than a Kubernetes-native scheduled job. D-03 leaves two
things explicitly unconfirmed: whether the lock utility is present in the container image, and
whether an outbound heartbeat ping is acceptable from the eventual AWS deployment. This plan
verifies what can be verified and labels honestly what cannot.

Output: three evidence subsections under `### Scheduling track (SCHED-07)` and one
`### SCHED-07` recommendation subsection in `31-DECISION.md`.
</objective>
