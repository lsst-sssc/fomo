## Accomplishments

- `src/fomo/settings.py` has `import sys` and a block `if len(sys.argv) > 1 and sys.argv[1] == 'test':` that sets `CACHES` to a LocMemCache. ## Decisions Made

- Race fixed at the cause (a private cache per test process) rather than retried or loosened.

- Config-file form of the coverage settings is enough (smoke test passed).
- tblib added after the developer's explicit approval.
