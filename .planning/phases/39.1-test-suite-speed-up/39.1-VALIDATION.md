## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** the task's own `<automated>` targeted run; each code commit also runs the pre-commit `django-test` hook in the background (serial, several minutes, until 39.1-02 Task 3; parallel and about a minute and a half after it)
- **After every plan wave:** the whole unit suite -- 39.1-01 Task 2 runs it serially; 39.1-02 Task 3 runs it three times with `--parallel` plus the new hook entry; 39.1-03 Task 3 runs it in five configurations (rows H, S, P, N, C4 of 39.1-TIMINGS.md)
- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:** the whole unit suite green under the exact hook entry and the exact CI command (39.1-03 Task 3), the five-run `--parallel 4` race loop green (39.1-02 Task 1), and the CI row of 39.1-TIMINGS.md filled or deferred by the developer (39.1-04)
- **Max feedback latency:** ~60 s for a targeted run; the before (~13 min) and after (~8 min) timing runs are background jobs, polled
