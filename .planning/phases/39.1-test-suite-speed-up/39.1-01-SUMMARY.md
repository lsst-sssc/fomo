## Accomplishments

- **SPEED-01:** `_cached_crossings(lon, lat, altitude, timezone, date, threshold)` is an `lru_cache(maxsize=4096)` helper called by `sun_event()` after its timezone, `to_earth_location()` and kind validation. A second identical call makes 0 `_solar_altitude` calls and 0 `_find_crossing` calls; returned `Time` objects are `.copy()` of the cached ones; two records with identical coordinates share an entry; each key component is its own entry; exceptions (blank timezone, coordinate-less site, unknown timezone, no-crossing ValueError) are never answered from or stored in the cache incorrectly. The 28 `patch(...sun_event...)` tests pass with their modules byte-identical to 128bb77.
- **SPEED-02:** `_find_crossing()` now does one 145-sample coarse transform plus 14 vectorised bisection steps: 15 transforms and 173 samples per two-crossing night (was 21 and 1,461).
- **Accuracy proof:** the pre-phase algorithm survives verbatim as `_dense_scan_reference` in the test module. Over Magellan-Clay, NTT and FTS x kinds sun/dark x 2026-04-05, 2026-06-21, 2026-12-21 the maximum |new - reference| is **0.044 s** (limit 1.0 s; skycalc contract 120 s). At the 59.9 N grazing site the reference gives 2 crossings on 2026-04-30 and 0 on 2026-05-01, and the new search matches both (max 0.037 s on the two-crossing date).
- Pre-phase precision tests, the skycalc references (`LAS_CAMPANAS_SUN_REFERENCE_UTC`, `TWILIGHT_18DEG_JUN10_UTC`) and the 120 s tolerance helper are AST-identical to 128bb77 (47 definitions checked) and pass.
- **Paired notebook:** `telescope_runs_demo.ipynb` re-executed with output (execution counts 1..11, `nbsphinx execute: never` kept): new "Memoised crossing search" section showing 1 search + 1 hit for two identical calls, a mutated copy not reaching the next call, and a moved site being a new search; NTT 2026-06-10 sunset 21:58:44.890 (was 21:58:44.883); the dip line now prints `1.4211 deg` and `Las Campanas (2402 m): 1.4376 deg`.
