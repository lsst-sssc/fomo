<objective>
Close 27-REVIEW WR-01's remaining half: the `telescope_class` guard in `import_campaign_csv`
currently stops a re-import *blanking* a non-blank value, but a hand-corrected value can still
be silently overwritten by a different derived one. Mirror the `preserve_site` guard's shape,
including its per-row "say so" reporting (D-04).

Purpose: `telescope_class` is the campaign-level answer to "why does this run have no site",
and nothing in the codebase ever clears it once set — so a staff member who corrects one
expects the correction to survive the next sheet re-import, exactly as a
`repair_stale_campaign_run_sites` fix now survives. Today it does not, and the row is reported
as `unchanged`, which is indistinguishable from a row where nothing was attempted.
Output: a `preserve_telescope_class` decision computed beside its inputs, a widened pop, a
`telescope_class_preserved` counter and stderr line, tests pinning both directions, and the
runbook's re-import gotcha brought back in step with the code.
</objective>
