<objective>
Make coverage-gap analysis provenance-blind: count every observation on the campaign calendar,
not only the nights an approved `CampaignRun` window happens to span (GAPB-01, D-16..D-19).

Purpose: `claimed_dates()` today queries only `CampaignRun` rows — read in full this session,
it never touches `ObservationRecord`, `CalendarEventMeta` or `CampaignRunObservation`. A
classical or queue observation taken on a night no run window covers therefore reads as an open
gap even though the night was used. That is exactly the roadmap's Success Criterion 5.

Output: a second claim source inside `claimed_dates()`, a site-unknown count surfaced on the
gap page, and tests covering the union, the exclusions and the site-resolution ladder.

**Paired docs:** owned by plan 37-07 (wave 5). RESEARCH.md confirmed the runbook has **no**
coverage-gap section at all today — 37-07 writes one fresh rather than editing a phantom.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
**Noun now primary:** the *claim source* — `claimed_dates()` had exactly one (approved run
windows) and now has two (run windows plus observation blocks), a singular→plural seam.

**Decision: add-alongside.** The run-window source stays as-is and the observation source is
unioned into the same returned set; the function does not become a registry of pluggable claim
sources.

**Accepted debt:** the two sources are hard-coded side by side inside one function rather than
iterated over a list of sources, so a hypothetical third source (an ESO Phase-2 read-back, say)
means editing the function again rather than registering a source. That is cheap at two and
would only be worth generalising at three or more.

**What would force a promote:** a third genuine claim source, or a requirement that the gap page
let a visitor toggle which sources count. Neither exists in v2.4.

**Suggested invariant test:** the union property itself — a night covered by both a run window
and an observation block appears exactly once in `result['claimed_dates']`, whichever source is
evaluated first.
</assumption_delta_decision>

    - A record classified `OBSERVED` with a placed block on a night inside the requested range adds that night to the claimed set.

    - A record whose site cannot be resolved at all increments the site-unknown counter and adds nothing to the claimed set.
