<objective>
Collapse the three parallel status-prefix vocabularies into one module and route the one
hardcoded terminal-state check through it (STATUS-01, STATUS-02).

Purpose: the codebase's own comments say the three maps "must stay byte-identical" to each
other — that is the drift disease this phase cures. Every other deliverable in Phase 37
(tallies, the unused-night marker, the gap page's claim labels) consumes this module's
classifier and marker tables, so it lands first and alone in wave 1.

Output: `solsys_code/status_vocabulary.py` plus its test module, with the projector, the
reconciler, the allocation projector, `calendar_utils` and the calendar template tags all
reading from it.

`docs/notebooks/pre_executed/campaign_lifecycle_demo.ipynb`,

**Decision: promote.** `status_vocabulary` becomes the single primary definition; the three
local maps are deleted rather than kept alongside. Rationale: the maps already had to agree

developer database has been re-titled.

reachable from any producer is a member of `status_vocabulary.MARKER.values()` — so a future
producer that invents a marker fails the suite rather than drifting quietly.
