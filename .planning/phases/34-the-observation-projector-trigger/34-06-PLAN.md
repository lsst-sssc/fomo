developer database untouched.

live `updatestatus` proof the UAT needs.

recorded determination on the runbook.

confirmed, not re-litigated, during execution:** `docs/runbooks/telescope_runs_calendar.rst`

unchanged. The task below confirms this with grep and records it; it does not edit the page.
