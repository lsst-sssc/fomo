<objective>
Replace three separately-drifting "clear the link" writes with one shared helper, so a cleared
attribution never leaves a stale confirmation behind and never destroys anything else.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 4 says clearing `CalendarEventMeta.run` must remove only the
decoration — the event untouched, nothing deleted. Today three call sites clear the link three
different ways, and one of them (the reconciler's detach step) leaves the "confirmed by X at T"
audit fields pointing at an attribution that no longer exists. One helper is what makes the
criterion checkable in one place.

Output: `unlink_event_from_run()` in `campaign_utils.py`; the attribution-undo view, the
reconciler's detach step and the admin clear branch all routed through it; and the tests for the
detach step's new audit-clearing behaviour, the no-deletion guarantee, and the attribution queue's
unchanged treatment of observation-backed events.
</objective>

| staff form -> CalendarEventMeta clear branch | The admin change form is a second, independent write path onto the same row |
