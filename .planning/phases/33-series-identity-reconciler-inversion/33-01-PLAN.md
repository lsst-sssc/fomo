<objective>
Invert the campaign reconciler from an owner into an annotator, and make campaign
attribution visible at display time instead of by writing into the event.

Purpose: this is the ordering landmine the whole v2.4 milestone rests on. Until
`reconcile_run()` stops adopting and re-keying events outside `RUN:`, Phase 34's observation
projector cannot run beside the campaign layer without having its events stolen. This plan
is that inversion, plus the display-time decoration that replaces what the adopt path used
to achieve by writing.

Output: a `_night_already_attributed()` skip rule and a retired adopt/re-key path in
`campaign_reconciler.py`; a `ReconcileResult.skipped_nights` counter; a `campaign_decoration`
template tag; the extended "Attributed campaign run" modal block; and the reconciler and
template test coverage that proves an attributed event survives a sweep untouched.
</objective>
