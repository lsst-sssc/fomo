**Ownership safety (the milestone's central risk).** When Phase 28 staff confirm an attribution, `CalendarEventMeta.run` is set to that run's pk. If a *different* run's reconciler later touches that event, `_may_write()` returns `False` because the companion row's `run_id` does not match, and the write is blocked at `campaign_reconciler.py:267-268` and `:387-390`. The reconciler cannot clobber or orphan a staff-confirmed attribution.
